# Brand voice - Hotel Aurora

Copy this file to `brand-voice.md` and replace everything below with your
own property's voice. This is what `rules.brand_voice_lock`'s note points a
person back to before they ship a copy test - see `knowledge/README.md`.

## Tone

Warm and direct. We write the way a good front-desk manager talks: plain
sentences, no jargon, no false urgency. We say what a guest gets, not how
excited we are about it.

## Never

- Countdown-clock language ("Only 2 rooms left!", "Offer ends tonight!").
  Our booking engine already shows real availability - we do not need to
  invent pressure on top of it.
- Exclamation marks in button copy.
- "Luxury" as a stand-alone adjective. Say what is actually good about the
  room or the view instead.

## An example that sounds right

Hero button: "Find the perfect room for you" - names the outcome, no
pressure, matches how a person would actually say it at the desk.
